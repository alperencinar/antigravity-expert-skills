# Command: /screenshot-spec

Generate target copy and structure guidelines for App Store & Google Play screenshots based on product PRD requirements.

---

## 1. Copywriting Best Practices
*   **Loss Aversion Hook:** Highlight what users avoid (e.g. overpaying, wasting money) in the very first screen.
*   **Short Caption Rule:** Maximum 5-6 words. Banners are read in milliseconds.
*   **Action Verbs:** Start captions with clear actions where applicable (`Compare`, `Find`, `Get`).
*   **Legible Typography:** Use high-contrast color pairings. White text on dark primary brand backgrounds is recommended.

---

## 2. Standard 6-Screen Spec Structure

When defining screenshots, structure them as a user journey:

### Frame 1: The Hook (Core Benefit)
*   **Focus:** Immediate emotional trigger.
*   **TR Headline:** `AI Ekspertiz: Boşuna Ekspertiz Ücreti Ödemeyin!`
*   **EN Headline:** `AI Appraisal: Stop Wasting Money on Car Inspections`
*   **Visual:** Core outcome page or high-impact badge screen.

### Frame 2: Core Differentiator
*   **Focus:** How the app solves the problem differently.
*   **TR Headline:** `Usta, Galerici ve Alıcı Gözünden`
*   **EN Headline:** `3 Expert Perspectives in 1 App`
*   **Visual:** Main product dashboard showing core features or personas.

### Frame 3: Diligent Comparison (VS Mode)
*   **Focus:** Eliminating decision fatigue.
*   **TR Headline:** `İki İlanı Yan Yana Karşılaştırın`
*   **EN Headline:** `Compare Car Listings Side-by-Side`
*   **Visual:** Side-by-side feature or product comparison screen.

### Frame 4: Personalized Search / Discovery
*   **Focus:** Helping top-of-funnel/undecided users.
*   **TR Headline:** `Bütçene & İhtiyacına En Uygun Arabayı Bul`
*   **EN Headline:** `Find Your Perfect Car Match`
*   **Visual:** Preference collection or recommendation results.

### Frame 5: Financial Advantage / Negotiation
*   **Focus:** Immediate monetary gain.
*   **TR Headline:** `Pazarlıkta Elinizi Güçlendirin`
*   **EN Headline:** `Get Smart Negotiation Leverage`
*   **Visual:** Negotiation arguments, seller questions, or price checks.

### Frame 6: Risk Mitigation / Details
*   **Focus:** In-depth evaluation checklist.
*   **TR Headline:** `Ekspertiz Öncesi İlk Elemeyi Yapın`
*   **EN Headline:** `Your Pre-Inspection Filter`
*   **Visual:** Interactive checklist or deep-dive details.
